The first elected Bangsamoro parliament is seated and the transition's political question has shifted from election to governance. Abdulraof Macacua won the chief minister vote 51 to 27 over Murad Ebrahim on 30 October, named a 19-member cabinet on 5 November, and now leads a coalition that controls 46 of 80 seats while Murad's MILF faction becomes the opposition. Marcos and Murad committed to signing the exit agreement before 2028 and completing the fourth decommissioning phase by 2027. Violence remains bounded but renewing: Abu Sayyaf remnants killed 10 soldiers and an auxiliary in October, and the PNP reports 74 cases of leaderless youth extremism.

## The Open Question

Can Macacua make the first elected Bangsamoro government deliver before the 2027 budget and decommissioning deadlines, while the MILF split hardens into a permanent opposition?
