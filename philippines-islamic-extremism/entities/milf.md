# Moro Islamic Liberation Front (MILF)

The MILF is the largest and most politically sophisticated Moro insurgent movement in the Philippines. Founded in 1984 as a splinter from the MNLF, it fought for an independent Islamic state before negotiating autonomy, signed the Comprehensive Agreement on the Bangsamoro in 2014, and governed the transition through BARMM. It is now the parent of two rival parties and no longer a single actor.

## Key Actions

- **2014:** Signed the Comprehensive Agreement on the Bangsamoro with the government, trading independence for autonomy.
- **2019:** Took control of the Bangsamoro Transition Authority after the organic law was ratified.
- **2025:** Lost control of the chief ministership when Marcos appointed Macacua without consulting the Central Committee.
- **June 2026:** Suspended Macacua as BIAF chief of staff after he formed the Bangsamoro Federalist Party.
- **September 2026:** Removed Macacua and 40 other BIAF officers from command, eleven days before the election.

## Current Position

The MILF's political wing, Murad's UBJP, won 30 seats in the first elected parliament, one fewer than Macacua's BFP. Murad remains MILF chairman but leads the opposition in the chamber his movement created. The armed wing has been purged of BFP-aligned commanders, while decommissioning remains stalled and weapons stay in the hands of units whose loyalty is now contested.

## Constraints

- The BFP-UBJP split runs through the political leadership, the armed wing and local government.
- Decommissioning is incomplete, leaving former fighters dependent on a process the government has not funded.
- Commander Bravo's threat to return to armed struggle limits how far Murad can compromise without losing his base.
