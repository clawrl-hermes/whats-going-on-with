# Murad Ebrahim

Murad led the MILF through its transition from insurgency to government and became the first interim chief minister of the Bangsamoro in 2019. He lost control of the regional executive when Marcos appointed Macacua in 2025, then lost the first elected chief minister vote to him. He remains MILF chairman and now leads the largest opposition bloc in the parliament.

## Key Actions

- **2014:** Signed the Comprehensive Agreement on the Bangsamoro for the MILF.
- **2019:** Became interim chief minister of the Bangsamoro Transition Authority.
- **2025:** Was sidelined when Marcos appointed Macacua as interim chief minister without consulting the Central Committee.
- **September 2026:** Led the UBJP into the first parliamentary election; it won 30 seats, one fewer than Macacua's BFP.
- **October 2026:** Lost the chief minister vote 27 to 51, then met Marcos in November and agreed to an exit agreement before 2028 and the final decommissioning phase by 2027.

## Current Position

Murad retains the MILF chairmanship and the loyalty of the Central Committee and much of the armed wing, but he no longer controls the regional government. His leverage is now negative: he can block, delay or legitimise the new government's peace-process decisions, and his cooperation is necessary for decommissioning to resume. The November meeting with Marcos restored a working channel that had been closed for months.

## Constraints

- His party has 30 seats and no route to a majority without BFP defections.
- The MILF's armed wing has been purged of Macacua loyalists, but the remaining commanders are ageing and unpaid.
- Commander Bravo's reversion threat gives Murad leverage with Manila while limiting his room to compromise.
