# Abdulraof Macacua

Macacua is the first elected chief minister of the Bangsamoro and the person most responsible for the MILF's split. A former BIAF chief of staff, he was appointed interim chief minister by Marcos in 2025 without the MILF Central Committee's consent, then built the Bangsamoro Federalist Party around his own base. He won the chief ministership with a coalition assembled after the election, not with a party majority.

## Key Actions

- **March 2025:** Appointed interim chief minister by Marcos, bypassing the MILF Central Committee.
- **May 2026:** Formed the Bangsamoro Federalist Party and removed Mohagher Iqbal as education minister.
- **June 2026:** Suspended as BIAF chief of staff by the Central Committee; survived an ambush in August.
- **September 2026:** Removed from his BIAF command along with 40 other officers, eleven days before the election; his BFP won 31 seats, one more than Murad's UBJP.
- **October 2026:** Assembled a 46-seat coalition and was elected chief minister with 51 votes against 27 for Murad.

## Current Position

Macacua governs with a 19-member cabinet and a coalition that includes the Bangsamoro Grand Coalition, which held the balance after the vote. He has the legitimacy of an elected mandate and the advantage of incumbency, but his majority is built on post-election deals rather than party discipline. He has promised continuity on the peace process while presenting himself as a break from MILF control.

## Constraints

- His party holds 31 of 80 seats; the coalition is broader than his base and can fragment.
- The MILF Central Committee and Murad's 30-seat bloc sit in opposition and retain influence over the armed wing.
- Decommissioning, rido and the 2027 budget will test whether his government can deliver, not just negotiate.
