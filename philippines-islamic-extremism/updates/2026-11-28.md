- **The Bangsamoro Parliament writes the Opposition into its rules** — Resolution 01, adopted at 11:56 p.m. on 24 November, creates a Westminster-style Government of the Day of the 51 MPs who voted for Macacua and a statutory Opposition of the 27 who voted for Murad, with 25 per cent of committee seats reserved; the majority denied 196 of the Opposition's comments on the standing orders and accepted 13 ([MindaNews](https://mindanews.com/peace-process/2026/11/in-the-bangsamoro-parliament-its-government-of-the-day-for-the-majority-and-opposition-for-the-minority/)).
- **Sectoral seats come without sectoral power** — The six sectoral representatives elected on the UBJP ticket, covering settlers, women, youth, traditional leaders and ulama, were refused their own committees and proportional committee membership, so the sectors that the peace agreement reserved seats for now sit inside the Opposition with a 25 per cent floor and no chair ([MindaNews](https://mindanews.com/peace-process/2026/11/sectoral-reps-push-for-sectoral-committees-in-bangsamoro-parliament/)).
- **The Senate funds normalization while decommissioning stays suspended** — OPAPRU's ₱9.4-billion 2027 budget was approved on 27 November, covering the MILF and MNLF normalization tracks, former-combatant transformation and PAMANA projects, with Secretary Sarmiento saying the agency is still asking Congress for more to reach an exit agreement before 2028 ([OPAPRU](https://peace.gov.ph/2026/11/senate-approves-opaprus-php-9-4-billion-peace-investment/)).
- **A Lanao del Sur police chief is killed in a premeditated attack** — Maj. Razul Pandulo, 35, chief of the Bubong municipal police station, was shot dead in his Kidapawan City boarding house on 23 November; police formed a task group and are pursuing four suspects, in a killing that fits the pattern of targeted attacks on officials and judges rather than jihadist operations ([Inquirer](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/2346150/barmm-cops-family-condemn-killing-of-police-major-in-kidapawan)).
