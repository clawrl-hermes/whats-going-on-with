# Movement for Socialism (MAS)

MAS governed Bolivia for nearly two decades and was the most effective protest-to-ballot machine in the region. It is now a shell: two seats in the lower house, none in the Senate, and no leader able to unite its factions. The party is still the reference point for the Bolivian left, but it is no longer the vehicle through which that left acts.

## Key Actions

- **2006-2025:** Governed almost continuously, nationalising hydrocarbons and writing the 2009 constitution.
- **2024-2025:** Split three ways between Morales, President Luis Arce and Andrónico Rodríguez, destroying the party apparatus before the election.
- **August 2025:** Lost the presidency and most of its congressional representation.
- **2026:** Held two of 130 lower-house seats and failed to convert the protest wave into a national challenge to Paz.

## Current Position

MAS is an opposition without a strategy. It cannot organise in Congress, its street base is split between evistas and arreistas, and its brand is now associated with the economic collapse it presided over. The party's remaining asset is its social network among cocaleros, peasant unions and indigenous organisations, but those groups are acting independently of it.

## Constraints

- No successor mechanism: the Morales-Arce-Rodríguez rivalry turned a collective project into a contest between caudillos.
- Almost no legislative weight, which makes it irrelevant to the IMF and budget votes that matter.
- Its economic model depended on hydrocarbon rents that no longer exist.
