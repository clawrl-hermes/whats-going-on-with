# Priority Sources — Bolivia's Political Situation

These sources should be checked first during every source monitor run, before conducting broader Tavily searches. They are organized by category reflecting source value and trust level.

Compiled 2026-11-13 from the topic's existing source base and the high-signal source types in `domain-notes.md`. Sites marked "bot-blocked" often still work through search; if extraction fails, search the outlet by name.

---

## Regional Specialists (high trust)

These outlets provide dedicated coverage of Bolivia. Check each for new articles.

- **El Deber** — https://eldeber.com.bo
  Santa Cruz daily and Bolivia's largest newspaper. Strongest business, lowland and eastern-political coverage. Bot-blocked; use search if extraction fails.
- **Los Tiempos** — https://www.lostiempos.com
  Cochabamba daily with national reach. Good on Congress, the economy and protest movements.
- **Opinión** — https://www.opinion.com.bo
  Cochabamba daily. Politics, regional government and business.
- **La Razón** — https://www.la-razon.com
  La Paz daily. Political analysis, interviews and judicial coverage. Bot-blocked; use search.
- **Brújula Digital** — https://www.brujuladigital.net
  Independent digital outlet. Investigative reporting and political analysis.
- **Erbol** — https://erbol.com.bo
  Radio and digital network with provincial reach. Fast on roadblocks, rural conflict and local politics.
- **Correo del Sur** — https://correodelsur.com
  Sucre-based daily. Chuquisaca politics, the judiciary and indigenous marches.
- **MercoPress** — https://en.mercopress.com
  English-language South America wire. Quick summaries of Bolivian politics for non-Spanish readers. Bot-blocked; use search.

## Think Tanks & Research Institutions (analytical depth)

These sources provide longer-form analysis, policy recommendations, and original research. Useful for context and deepening understanding, not for breaking news.

- **Fundación Tierra** — https://ftierra.org
  Land, territory and rural-conflict research. The best source on indigenous, campesino and lowland disputes. Bot-blocked; use search.
- **CEDLA** — https://cedla.org
  Labour and economic policy research. Wages, informality, subsidies and the social aftermath of adjustment.
- **WOLA** — https://www.wola.org
  Washington Office on Latin America. Rights and drug-policy analysis; critical of US policy.
- **NACLA** — https://nacla.org
  Left-leaning analysis of US-Latin America relations. Useful for Morales, MAS and cocalero framing.
- **Americas Quarterly** — https://www.americasquarterly.org
  Political economy of the region. Coalition durability, governance and the rightward shift.
- **CSIS Americas Program** — https://www.csis.org/regions/americas
  US policy framing on Bolivia, China in Latin America and security.

## Official Sources (primary — biased, monitor for policy statements)

Direct statements from governments and institutions. Essential for tracking decrees, programme conditions and macro data. Treat as primary sources — the framing is always self-serving.

- **Presidencia de Bolivia** — https://www.presidencia.gob.bo
  Presidential speeches, cabinet announcements and decree presentations.
- **Banco Central de Bolivia** — https://www.bcb.gob.bo
  Reserves, exchange rate, monetary policy and financial-sector data.
- **Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE)** — https://www.ine.gob.bo
  Inflation, GDP, employment and trade statistics.
- **Ministerio de Relaciones Exteriores** — https://www.cancilleria.gob.bo
  Diplomatic statements, Chile relations and regional positions.
- **ASFI** — https://www.asfi.gob.bo
  Banking regulation, financial-sector data and institution supervision.
- **Ministerio de Hidrocarburos y Energías** — https://www.mhe.gob.bo
  Hydrocarbons policy, YPFB oversight and fuel-import decisions.
- **Aduana Nacional** — https://www.aduana.gob.bo
  Import and export data, smuggling cases and fuel-control measures.
- **IMF — Bolivia** — https://www.imf.org/en/Countries/BOL
  Programme reviews, disbursements and macro projections. Extract unconditionally when a search returns an IMF URL.
- **Gaceta Oficial de Bolivia** — https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.bo
  The legal record for decrees, including subsidy changes. The site is frequently unreachable; search the decree number instead.

## Advocacy & Civil Society (useful signal — biased framing)

These sources represent specific constituencies. Useful for tracking rights abuses, judicial independence and coca policy. Always cross-reference with regional specialists.

- **Human Rights Watch — Bolivia** — https://www.hrw.org/americas/bolivia
  Rights reporting on the justice system, protests and indigenous communities.
- **Amnesty International — Bolivia** — https://www.amnesty.org/en/location/americas/south-america/bolivia/
  Campaigns and reports on repression, impunity and freedom of expression.
- **CEDIB** — https://www.cedib.org
  Documentation centre on coca, drug policy and security. Strong primary-document archive.
- **Fundación Construir** — https://www.fundacionconstruir.org
  Judicial independence, access to justice and legal reform.

## Source Selection Guidance

1. **Check regional specialists first** — visit/extract each for new articles since the last run
2. **Then check official sources** — scan for new decrees, programme decisions, reserves data or diplomatic statements
3. **Then check think tanks** — for analytical context on recent developments
4. **Then check advocacy sources** — for rights, judicial and coca-policy allegations
5. **Finally, run Tavily searches** — to catch anything the curated sources missed
