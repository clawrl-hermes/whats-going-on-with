# Timeline: Bolivia's Political Situation

## 2025
- **August:**
  - Rodrigo Paz wins the presidential runoff against Jorge Quiroga, campaigning as a centrist.
- **November:**
  - Paz is inaugurated as Bolivia's first right-wing president in decades, inheriting fuel shortages, depleted reserves and rising inflation.

## 2026
- **May:**
  - Protests spread as Indigenous, rural and transport groups block roads and demand Paz resign over the cancellation of fuel subsidies.
  - Blockades paralyse La Paz, El Alto and Santa Cruz, producing food, fuel and oxygen shortages.
- **June:**
  - The government signs an agreement with the Bolivian Workers' Confederation on 19 June, the first step toward ending the blockade.
  - Paz declares a state of emergency on 20 June and deploys the military to clear roads; at least 17 people are killed.
  - The Legislative Assembly ratifies the emergency decree and blockades lift by 21 June.
  - Bolivia ends its 15-year dollar peg; the boliviano is devalued by about 30% to 9.73 per dollar.
- **July:**
  - The IMF reaches a staff-level agreement on a $1.9 billion, three-year Extended Fund Facility on 29 July.
  - A second arrest warrant is issued for Evo Morales, on armed insurrection and terrorism charges.
- **August:**
  - Supreme Decree 5676 raises diesel prices for large consumers by 83.7%; the government concedes within days that it must be annulled.
  - Congress censures Economy Minister José Gabriel Espinoza over the economic programme.
  - Fernando Cerimedo, Paz's campaign adviser, is arrested over the shooting of activist Nadia Beller, exposing the unreleased Decree 5503.
  - Christian Morales replaces Espinoza; the National Unity party leaves the coalition.
  - Only six of the original fifteen ministers remain in post.
- **September:**
  - The government places YPFB under a 180-day state intervention over the fuel shortage.
  - Congress approves the $1.9 billion IMF programme on 18 September.
  - Supreme Decree 5716 ends the remaining diesel subsidy, raising the price 83% to Bs17.95 per litre.
  - The state of exception is extended for 90 days.
  - Morales gives the government until 3 October to repeal the diesel decrees.
- **October:**
  - The IMF Board approves the facility on 2 October, releasing $214 million immediately.
  - Attorney General Roger Mariaca is arrested after the US revokes his visa.
  - The diesel price rise triggers the "dieselazo".
  - Chilean President José Antonio Kast makes the first Chilean state visit to Bolivia in 71 years.
  - Paz declares a nationwide El Niño emergency.
- **November:**
  - Paz travels to Madrid for the Ibero-American Summit to court European investment.
