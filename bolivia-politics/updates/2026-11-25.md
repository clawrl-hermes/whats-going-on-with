- **A market-priced diesel raises the cost of the adjustment** — The ANH lifted diesel from Bs17.95 to Bs20.57 a litre at midnight on 24 November, Bs2.62 or 14.6%, applying the international-price formula written into Decree 5716 rather than a fresh subsidy decision. Hydrocarbons Minister Marcelo Blanco called the move "normal" and said the price could fall again, but gasoline remains at Bs6.96 until its own phase-out in January. ([Brújula Digital](https://brujuladigital.net/economia/2026/11/24/transportistas-se-movilizan-contra-el-dieselazo-bloqueos-en-oruro-y-chuquisaca-y-paro-en-yungas-69601))
- **The diesel rise produces blockades in nine departments within a day** — Truckers blocked the Oruro road to Potosí and Chuquisaca, Yungas carriers suspended departures for 48 hours, and Chuquisaca's federation opened an indefinite strike with roughly 70 blockade points in Sucre from 25 November. Tanker drivers began withdrawing about 5,000 units over unpaid October bills from YPFB, threatening fuel logistics ahead of the 26 November national plenary of the drivers' confederation. ([Visión 360](https://www.vision360.bo/noticias/2026/11/24/78810-alza-del-diesel-provoca-protestas-en-nueve-departamentos-anuncian-bloqueos-paros-y-repliegue-de-cisternas), [Brújula Digital](https://brujuladigital.net/economia/2026/11/24/transportistas-se-movilizan-contra-el-dieselazo-bloqueos-en-oruro-y-chuquisaca-y-paro-en-yungas-69601))
- **Deputies finally constitute their commissions, six weeks into the term** — The Chamber of Deputies approved 12 permanent commissions and 37 committees by a two-thirds vote on 18 November after a session broken by repeated intermissions, giving the budget and the investment bill a committee route. The Senate had formed its 10 commissions days earlier, with the governing PDC chairing four, Alianza Libre four and Alianza Unidad two. ([Prensa Latina](https://www.prensa-latina.cu/2026/11/18/conforma-comisiones-legislativo-en-bolivia))
- **The 2027 budget is heading for approval by default rather than by vote** — Presented on 30 October, the bill was only just referred to the Planning Commission, and deputies from both Libre and the PDC expect no floor vote before the December recess. Under the 60-day rule an unvoted budget is deemed approved and published by decree, so the 6.7%-of-GDP deficit and the Bs11bn gasoline subsidy would take effect without Congress deciding either. ([El Deber](https://eldeber.com.bo/economia/retraso-legislativo-pone-pge-2027-posible-vigencia-aval-alp_1795170617))
- **Paz's own party loses its legal registration** — The TSE cancelled the PDC's legal personality for failing to file financial statements since 2023, a sanction opened in December 2025 that bars the party from future elections. The PDC carried Paz and Edmand Lara to power and chairs four Senate commissions, so the ruling strips the government of its electoral vehicle while leaving its legislative bloc intact. ([El Deber](https://eldeber.com.bo/pais/partido-llevo-poder-paz-queda-personeria_1795603705))
- **Inflation is tracking far below the budget's own assumption** — BCB president David Espinoza put January–October inflation at 7.1% and projected 9.2% for the year, against 20.4% in 2025, crediting the move to a flexible exchange rate. That is nearly a point and a half below the 10.7% the 2027 budget assumes, weakening the revenue case the government used to justify its deficit. ([Infodiez](https://www.infodiez.com/banco-central-proyecta-una-inflacion-del-92-para-el-cierre-de-2026))
