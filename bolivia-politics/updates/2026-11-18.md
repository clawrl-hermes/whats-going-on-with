- **An antidrug ambush turns the Chapare into an open cartel dispute** — An Umopar unit was ambushed before dawn on 11 November in Villa Tunari during "Operación Muralla", wounding three officers including the unit's national commander; Government Minister Marco Antonio Oviedo used the same day to call Morales the head of a Chapare cartel that financed the May–June blockades, and Morales answered by renaming him "Narco Antonio Oviedo". Federation leaders reported gassing and point-blank shooting at the El Castillo checkpoint and claimed Israeli intelligence had entered the Trópico. ([Correo del Sur](https://correodelsur.com/politica/20261112/evo-llama-narco-a-oviedo-tras-emboscada-de-cocaleros.html), [Erbol](https://www.erbol.com.bo/seguridad/evismo-dice-que-la-inteligencia-israel%C3%AD-mossad-ingres%C3%B3-al-tr%C3%B3pico))
- **Prosecutors are asked to seek an Interpol red notice for Morales** — The Procuraduría asked on 13 November that the Las Américas case be widened to murder charges against Morales and Álvaro García Linera as alleged intellectual authors, and that a red notice be issued on the strength of Morales's two live warrants; police also received a request to process a blue notice. García Linera, who holds Mexican asylum, is to be summonsed through diplomatic channels. ([El Deber](https://eldeber.com.bo/pais/caso-hotel-americas-procuraduria-pide-investigar-evo-morales-alvaro-garcia-linera-asesinato_1794620604))
- **The Chamber of Deputies still cannot form its committees** — Deputies went into another intermission on 17 November with the 2026–27 committee lists unagreed, the session running from just after 16:00 until 21:12; Speaker Roberto Castro said consensus was harder than last year and kept the directive in permanent session. Until the lists are voted the chamber cannot fully process legislation, including the 2027 budget that is meant to be settled by 30 November. ([La Razón](https://larazon.bo/nacional/2026/11/17/comisiones-diputados-van-a-cuarto-intermedio-por-falta-de-acuerdos))
- **$750 million of IDB credit is authorised by decree** — Supreme Decrees 5769 and 5770 let the Economy Ministry sign two IDB contracts, $500m for macroeconomic and fiscal stability and $250m for fiscal sustainability, but the funds reach the single Treasury account only once the Assembly ratifies them. ([La Razón](https://larazon.bo/economia-y-empresa/2026/11/17/gobierno-autoriza-prestamo-de-us-500-mm-del-bid-para-estabilidad-macroeconomica))
- **Both capitals move to fill their empty embassies** — The White House sent Kara Frederick's nomination to the Senate on 16 November, the first US ambassador to Bolivia since 2008, and Paz proposed former employers'-confederation president Giovanni Ortuño for Washington the next day. Both nominations now depend on Senate confirmation in Washington and approval in La Paz. ([Latin Times](https://www.latintimes.com/trump-sends-senate-tech-security-expert-fill-bolivias-long-empty-ambassador-post-601477), [El Deber](https://eldeber.com.bo/pais/giovanni-ortuno-propuesta-rodrigo-paz-embajada-bolivia-estados-unidos_1794962780))
- **The suitcases scandal yields its first convictions** — Aldo Quezada, former president of Santa Cruz's departmental court, and ex-antidrug prosecutor Julio César Porras admitted criminal-organisation charges on 13 November and drew three-year sentences to be served outside prison over at least 32 suitcases that entered Viru Viru without control. Romer Saucedo, the exiled Supreme Court president named in the case, asked Paz on 14 November to stop "this persecution" and denied ever commanding the DGAC, Naabol, Customs or the FELCN. ([El Deber](https://eldeber.com.bo/pais/quezada-porras-primeros-condenados-caso-maletas-rojas-rechaza-juicio-abreviado_1794615594), [Correo del Sur](https://correodelsur.com/seguridad/20261115/saucedo-pide-a-paz-frenar-la-persecucion-en-su-contra.html))
