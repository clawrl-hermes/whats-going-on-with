# Bidzina Ivanishvili

Bidzina Ivanishvili is Georgia's richest man and the informal author of Georgian Dream's strategy. He founded the party in 2012 and has alternated between formal leadership and backstage control since, holding no office after 2013 but remaining the final arbiter of the party and the state.

## Key Actions

- **2012** — Founds Georgian Dream, wins parliamentary elections and serves as prime minister.
- **2024** — Backs the foreign agents law and the disputed parliamentary elections.
- **2025–2026** — Western sanctions target him and senior Georgian Dream officials.
- **August 2026** — Jailed former prime minister Gharibashvili accuses him of blackmail.
- **September 2026** — Trump Tower Tbilisi is unveiled on land linked to him.
- **2026** — Drives the Constitutional Court case to ban the major opposition parties.

## Current Position

Ivanishvili remains the ultimate decision-maker, but his position is more contested than at any point since 2012. Sanctions restrict his travel and finance, a former prime minister has publicly broken with him, and the Trump Tower project is his main channel to Washington. His method is indirect: officials carry the public argument while he controls financing and appointments, which makes him hard to sanction effectively and hard to confront directly. He is using Kobakhidze as a buffer while the opposition ban and the economy determine whether the system holds.

## Constraints

- Western sanctions and asset freezes limit his financial and diplomatic room.
- His strategy depends on Russia not escalating beyond economic pressure.
- Internal rivals and public fatigue erode his control over the party.
