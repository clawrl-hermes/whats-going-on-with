Georgia has restored a direct channel to Moscow without resolving the contradiction in its foreign policy. Tbilisi wants trade and transport relief from Russia while keeping its EU and US options open, but Brussels has frozen accession and Washington's attention is conditional. The channel is narrow, a special representative rather than diplomatic relations, and the government still rules out normalisation while Russia occupies the territories. The binding constraint is domestic: the opposition ban and the budget will decide whether the West treats Georgia as a partner or a problem.

## The Open Question

Can Georgian Dream keep the Moscow channel open without crossing the EU and US red lines on occupation and political pluralism?
