# Timeline: Georgia — Geopolitical Positioning

## 2008
- **August:**
  - Russia defeats Georgia in a five-day war and recognises Abkhazia and South Ossetia, occupying about 20% of Georgian territory.

## 2022
- **February:**
  - Russia launches its full-scale invasion of Ukraine.
- **March:**
  - Georgia applies for EU membership alongside Ukraine and Moldova.

## 2023
- **December:**
  - The EU grants Georgia candidate status.

## 2024
- **May:**
  - The foreign agents law passes amid mass protests in Tbilisi.
- **October:**
  - Georgian Dream wins disputed parliamentary elections.
- **November:**
  - Georgia suspends EU accession talks, and the US suspends its strategic partnership with Tbilisi.

## 2025
- **March:**
  - Armenia passes an EU membership law.
- **Spring:**
  - Protests against the government pass 500 days.
- **August:**
  - Armenia and Azerbaijan move toward a peace agreement.

## 2026
- **June:**
  - The Baku-Tbilisi-Kars railway reopens.
  - Georgia and China upgrade to a comprehensive strategic partnership.
- **July:**
  - China exits the Anaklia deep-sea port project; Georgia adopts a state-led "landlord" model.
  - Kobakhidze tours Central Asia, signing partnerships with Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan.
- **August:**
  - Georgia refuses to join EU sanctions on Iran.
  - The White House names Georgia in China's tariff-circumvention network.
- **September:**
  - Georgian Dream moves to ban all major opposition parties through the Constitutional Court.
  - Marat Kambolov, a former Russian official, wins South Ossetia's presidential election with 85%.
  - A US court orders Georgia to pay $350 million over the abandoned Namakhvani hydropower project.
  - Hungary's new government shifts toward mainstream EU positions.
- **October:**
  - Russia bans imports from seven Georgian companies.
- **November:**
  - The European Commission's enlargement report says Georgia's accession is "de facto halted".
