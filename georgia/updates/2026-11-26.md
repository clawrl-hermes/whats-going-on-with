- **Tbilisi and Moscow hold the first talks under the upgraded channel** — Zurabashvili met Galuzin in Minsk on 19 November, the first meeting since the special representative's appointment, and both sides reported "practical agreements" without disclosing any. Tbilisi's readout named the Geneva International Discussions as the main format; Moscow's did not mention them. ([Civil.ge](https://civil.ge/archives/761208), [OC Media](https://oc-media.org/georgian-foreign-minister-urges-country-to-overcome-fear-of-speaking-with-moscow-following-minsk-talks))
- **The foreign minister makes dialogue with Moscow official policy** — Botchorishvili said Georgia must "overcome the fear of talking" to the country with which it has the biggest problems, and Lavrov answered on 25 November that Russia is "interested in normalizing relations", while Belarus's foreign minister floated moving the talks to Tbilisi and Moscow. ([OC Media](https://oc-media.org/georgian-foreign-minister-urges-country-to-overcome-fear-of-speaking-with-moscow-following-minsk-talks), [Civil.ge](https://civil.ge/archives/761928))
- **Brussels offers to lift the Kulevi sanction it imposed in July** — the Commission's proposed 22nd package recommends removing the refinery and its parent Black Sea Petroleum from the lists after they switched to Libyan crude, roughly 352,000 tonnes in three months, and cites Kulevi as proof that the threat of sanctions changes behaviour. Removal still needs unanimity. ([OC Media](https://oc-media.org/eu-commission-recommends-removal-of-sanctions-from-georgias-kulevi-oil-refinery))
- **The EU drafts its regional transport format without Georgia** — Enlargement Commissioner Marta Kos has proposed a corridor framework covering Armenia, Azerbaijan and Turkey that leaves Georgia out, even as Georgia–EU trade reached a record USD 5.6 billion in 2025. EU foreign ministers are due to discuss Georgia in January. ([bne IntelliNews](https://www.intellinews.com/europe-bets-on-a-peace-corridor-in-the-south-caucasus-486838))
- **The government dismantles the grants agency it built to fund friendly civil society** — a decree signed on 24 November dissolves the State Grants Management Agency by 1 January 2027, closing the channel set up under the 2025 grants law with GEL 20 million behind it, while the Venice Commission still calls for the law to be revised. ([JAMnews](https://jam-news.net/georgia-to-abolish-state-grants-management-agency-what-do-we-know-about-decision))
- **A sanctioned Russian marketplace builds logistics depth inside Georgia** — Wildberries' local operator bought 19.25 hectares of state land beside the Tbilisi–Red Bridge highway, leases a 12,800-square-metre warehouse and is preparing a taxi launch, with an owner who donated to Georgian Dream and a predecessor on Cartu Bank's board; the EU sanctioned founder Tatyana Kim on 16 November. ([JAMnews](https://jam-news.net/sanctioned-russian-online-retailer-wildberries-expands-operations-in-georgia))
