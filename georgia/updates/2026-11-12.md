- **China sends its most senior official yet to Tbilisi** — Vice President Han Zheng visited on 6–8 November, the highest-ranking Chinese official ever to visit Georgia by the government's own account, and met Kavelashvili and Kobakhidze. Kobakhidze called China "the only peaceful superpower in the modern world", thanked Beijing for backing Georgia's sovereignty and reaffirmed the One China principle; Han promised to buy more Georgian products, but no new agreements were signed. ([OC Media](https://oc-media.org/the-only-peaceful-superpower-kobakhidze-praises-china-during-vice-presidents-visit), [SpecialEurasia](https://www.specialeurasia.com/2026/11/07/georgia-china-han-zheng-visit))
- **The EU is now Georgia's weakest bilateral relationship, below Russia** — A Clingendael survey of 41 regional specialists scored Georgia–EU relations 2.57 out of 10 against 3.71 for Georgia–Russia, putting the EU last of 16 partners while Armenia, at 8.47, became the EU's closest partner in the region. The authors conclude Tbilisi lost its Western ties without securing comparable replacements, its strongest relations now being Turkey, Azerbaijan, the UAE and China. ([bne IntelliNews](https://www.intellinews.com/armenia-replaces-georgia-as-eu-s-closest-south-caucasus-partner-481930))
- **Moscow's security chief endorses Georgian Dream and revives the 3+3 push** — Sergei Shoigu told CIS security council secretaries on 9 November that the government "practically prevented a coup" in 2024 and now defends national interests, welcoming trade growth and calling for the 3+3 platform's resumption — a format Georgia has refused to join. ([OC Media](https://oc-media.org/russian-official-praises-georgian-dream-claiming-they-prevented-a-coup-in-2024))
- **Georgia's Russian exposure is smaller overall but deeper in gas** — A BM.ge study puts revenue from Russian exports, tourism and remittances at 5% of GDP in 2025, down from 14.4% in 2022, yet gas imports from Russia rose from 397 to 886 million cubic metres between 2021 and 2025 and Russia still takes about 64% of Georgian wine exports. ([JAMnews](https://jam-news.net/which-sectors-of-georgias-economy-are-most-dependent-on-russia))
- **The court bars two opposition leaders from the party-ban hearings** — On 11 November Constitutional Court chairman Revaz Nadaraia removed Nika Gvaramia and Maia Kopaleishvili from the courtroom for arguing with the bench, barring them from further hearings unless readmitted; a ruling is due by 24 June 2027 and needs four of seven judges. ([JAMnews](https://jam-news.net/georgia-opposition-ban-trial-court-removes-leaders-of-coalition-for-change))
- **Kobakhidze goes to Brussels without an accession plan** — In a 40-minute Euronews interview on 3 November he restated the goal of EU membership but offered no steps to restart talks, accused the Commission of political bias and "blackmail", and said Brussels should change its approach instead. ([Georgia Today](https://georgiatoday.ge/kobakhidze-offers-no-plan-to-revive-georgias-eu-accession-process-after-brussels-interview))
