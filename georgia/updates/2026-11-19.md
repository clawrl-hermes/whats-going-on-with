- **Brussels leaves Georgia out of its enlargement showcase** — The Commission announced on 9 November that Georgia would not be invited to the 15–16 November Enlargement Forum, which convened the other candidates in Brussels while Tbilisi's accession remains de facto halted. ([EUobserver](https://euobserver.com/249198/interview-georgia-has-reached-final-stage-of-autocratic-consolidation-warns-former-deputy-foreign-minister))

([JAMnews](https://jam-news.net/imf-deteriorating-relations-between-georgia-and-eu-could-dampen-investor-confidence/))
- **The Democratic sweep reopens the MEGOBARI path** — With both chambers in Democratic hands the Senate blockade that kept the bill from a vote is gone on paper, and OC Media judges passage likely, which would cement the freeze with Georgian Dream rather than lift sanctions. ([OC Media](https://oc-media.org/explainer-what-could-the-us-midterms-mean-for-the-caucasus))
