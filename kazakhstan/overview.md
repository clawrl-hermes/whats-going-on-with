---
title: Kazakhstan's Global Competitiveness
tagline: A middle power balancing great powers while trying to diversify.
region: Central Asia
started: 2026-06-04
---

This topic tracks Kazakhstan's attempt to turn geography, energy and minerals into durable economic and strategic advantage. Under Kassym-Jomart Tokayev, Astana has moved from balancing between Russia and the West to presenting itself as a regional organizer and connector state.

The through-line is diversification. Oil still dominates exports and the Caspian Pipeline Consortium remains the main export route, but Kazakhstan is expanding alternative corridors, building a manufacturing base and betting on AI and digital services. Each of those bets pulls it deeper into competition between Washington, Beijing and Moscow.

Coverage follows the multi-vector strategy, the CPC bottleneck and route diversification, China and Russia ties, the EU and US relationship, the AI pivot, and the domestic political reform that underpins it all.
