- **Budget lands on a $70 oil assumption as National Fund inflows thin** — The Kurultai adopted the 2027-2029 budget, adding 47 billion tenge for 52 regional infrastructure projects, but the plan assumes $70 a barrel, down from $75, and oil inflows to the National Fund are projected 342 billion tenge below 2026 levels as output nears a 2027-2028 peak ([Akorda](https://www.akorda.kz/en/president-tokayev-reviews-legislative-priorities-with-kuryltai-chairman-aibek-dadebay-23104637), [Eurasia Review](https://www.eurasiareview.com/19112026-in-fortress-mode-what-really-lies-behind-the-personnel-purges-and-arrests-in-kazakhstan-analysis)).
- **Non-oil energy exports fall into deficit as oil output drops** — Energy exports were $35.5 billion in January-September, but strip out crude and exports fell 25% to $1.8 billion while imports rose 44% to $2.6 billion, an $832 million deficit; oil production fell 8.3% to 69.4 million tons over the same period ([Astana Times](https://astanatimes.com/2026/11/iea-says-hormuz-shock-could-reshape-oil-trade-for-years)).
- **KTZ gets a new chairman and its listing slips to 2027** — Samruk-Kazyna replaced detained rail chairman Yelzhas Otynshiyev on 24 November, and advisers recommended moving the Hong Kong, London and Astana IPO into 2027 citing market conditions, six days after KTZ cleared its Hong Kong listing hearing ([Times of Central Asia](https://timesca.com/kazakhstan-appoints-new-ktz-chief-as-ipo-faces-possible-delay)).
- **The corruption probe reaches Kazatomprom** — The anti-corruption service detained management board member Kuanysh Omarbekov on 23 November over a scheme allegedly organised by former Samruk-Kazyna management, and the uranium producer's board removed him and chief commercial officer Vladislav Baiguzhin the next day while insisting guidance was unchanged ([Trend.az](https://www.trend.az/casia/kazakhstan/4250471.html)).
- **Tokayev goes to Moscow for AI, not for cover** — At the Kremlin on 25 November Tokayev and Putin pledged cooperation in artificial intelligence and high-tech industry as bilateral trade approaches $30 billion, while Kazakh crude still moves overwhelmingly through Russia: 64.8 million tons via the CPC in 2025, over 82% of exports ([Astana Times](https://astanatimes.com/2026/11/tokayev-putin-focus-on-ai-cooperation-as-bilateral-trade-nears-30-billion), [Times of Central Asia](https://timesca.com/beyond-moscow-kazakhstans-push-for-economic-and-technological-independence)).
- **Connectivity expands, but the new cable carries geopolitical strings** — The 380-kilometre Aktau-Sumgayit cable entered testing with 400 Tbps of capacity and hopes of 21.6 Tbps of transit traffic, yet its supplier HMN Technologies is on the US Entity List, operator CaspiLink has no repair vessel and Russia controls the Caspian's main access waterway; separately, Kazakhstan handles 84% of China-Central Asia freight, and five Caspian states began work on cross-border QR payments ([Eurasianet](https://eurasianet.org/commentary-azerbaijan-and-kazakhstan-launch-trans-caspian-digital-cable), [Astana Times](https://astanatimes.com/2026/11/kazakhstan-handles-84-of-china-central-asia-freight-as-regional-trade-nearly-triples), [Eurasianet](https://eurasianet.org/central-asian-states-striving-to-improve-electricity-distribution-network)).
