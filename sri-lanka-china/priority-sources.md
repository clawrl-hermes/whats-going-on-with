# Priority Sources — Sri Lanka-China Relations

These sources should be checked first during every source monitor run, before conducting broader Tavily searches. They are organized by category reflecting source value and trust level.

Compiled 2026-11-13 from the topic's existing source base and the high-signal source types in `domain-notes.md`. Sites marked "bot-blocked" often still work through search; if extraction fails, search the outlet by name.

---

## Regional Specialists (high trust)

These outlets provide dedicated coverage of Sri Lanka. Check each for new articles.

- **EconomyNext** — https://economynext.com
  Sri Lanka's best business and economic news outlet. Debt restructuring, IMF programme, reserves and port economics; the most consistent source of restructuring detail.
- **Daily FT** — https://www.ft.lk
  Business daily. Budget coverage, official interviews and investment announcements.
- **Daily Mirror** — https://www.dailymirror.lk
  Mass-market daily. Politics, official statements and parliamentary coverage.
- **Sunday Times** — https://www.sundaytimes.lk
  Weekend paper with stronger political and economic analysis than the dailies.
- **The Morning** — https://www.themorning.lk
  Colombo daily covering politics, business and diplomacy.
- **Newswire** — https://www.newswire.lk
  Fast online news service. Official announcements and breaking developments.
- **News 1st** — https://www.newsfirst.lk
  TV and digital newsroom. Fast incident coverage and interviews.
- **Tamil Guardian** — https://www.tamilguardian.com
  Tamil diaspora outlet. Human rights, land and the North-East's experience of Chinese and Indian projects. Campaigning framing; cross-check facts.

## Think Tanks & Research Institutions (analytical depth)

These sources provide longer-form analysis, policy recommendations, and original research. Useful for context and deepening understanding, not for breaking news.

- **Institute of Policy Studies of Sri Lanka (IPS)** — https://www.ips.lk
  Sri Lanka's main economic policy research institute. Debt, trade, poverty and fiscal analysis.
- **Verité Research** — https://www.veriteresearch.org
  Independent research and polling. Budget analysis, governance and public-opinion data.
- **Advocata Institute** — https://www.advocata.org
  Market-liberal think tank. Reform proposals and commentary on fiscal and trade policy.
- **Lowy Institute** — https://www.lowyinstitute.org
  Australian institute with sustained analysis of Chinese lending and the Belt and Road.
- **AidData** — https://www.aiddata.org
  Academic lab producing the most authoritative data on Chinese loan amounts, terms and lenders.
- **CSIS** — https://www.csis.org
  Reconnecting Asia project. Chinese infrastructure, port projects and Indian Ocean strategy.

## Official Sources (primary — biased, monitor for policy statements)

Direct statements from governments and institutions. Essential for tracking restructuring terms, programme reviews and diplomatic positioning. Treat as primary sources — the framing is always self-serving.

- **Sri Lanka Ministry of Finance / Public Debt Management Office** — https://www.treasury.gov.lk
  Debt bulletins, investor presentations and restructuring data. The best official source on creditor composition and debt sustainability.
- **Central Bank of Sri Lanka** — https://www.cbsl.gov.lk
  Reserves, monetary policy, balance of payments and financial-sector data.
- **Ministry of Foreign Affairs** — https://www.mfa.gov.lk
  Diplomatic statements and bilateral engagements.
- **Sri Lanka Ports Authority** — https://www.slpa.lk
  Port operations, Colombo and Hambantota throughput, and terminal concessions.
- **Board of Investment** — https://www.investsrilanka.com
  Investment approvals and project announcements, including Chinese-backed ventures.
- **International Monetary Fund — Sri Lanka** — https://www.imf.org/en/Countries/LKA
  Programme reviews, staff reports and disbursements. Extract unconditionally when a search returns an IMF URL.
- **China Ministry of Commerce (MOFCOM)** — http://english.mofcom.gov.cn
  Chinese trade and investment data, and contract announcements.
- **India Ministry of External Affairs** — https://www.mea.gov.in
  Indian positions on Sri Lanka, debt relief and connectivity competition.

## Advocacy & Civil Society (useful signal — biased framing)

These sources represent specific constituencies. Useful for tracking corruption, environmental disputes and rights allegations. Always cross-reference with regional specialists.

- **Transparency International Sri Lanka** — https://www.tisrilanka.org
  Corruption and governance research. Procurement, transparency and public-finance accountability.
- **Centre for Environmental Justice** — https://ejustice.lk
  Environmental justice advocacy. Coal plants, land acquisition and port-related impacts.
- **Environmental Foundation Ltd** — https://www.efl.lk
  Environmental litigation and commentary on impact assessments.
- **International Truth and Justice Project** — https://itjpsl.com
  Transitional justice and human rights documentation. Diaspora-based, advocacy framing.

---

## Source Selection Guidance

1. **Check regional specialists first** — visit/extract each for new articles since the last run
2. **Then check official sources** — scan for new debt data, programme decisions, port announcements or diplomatic statements
3. **Then check think tanks** — for analytical context on recent developments
4. **Then check advocacy sources** — for corruption, environmental and rights allegations
5. **Finally, run Tavily searches** — to catch anything the curated sources missed
