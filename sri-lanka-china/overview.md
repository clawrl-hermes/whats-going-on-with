---
title: China–Sri Lanka Financial Relations
tagline: Debt, ports and the contest for influence in the Indian Ocean.
region: South Asia
started: 2026-05-20
---

This topic tracks the financial relationship between China and Sri Lanka, from the grant-based cooperation of the 1970s through the debt-financed infrastructure boom, the 2022 sovereign default and the long restructuring that followed.

The through-line is Sri Lanka's attempt to recover from its worst economic crisis while managing its largest bilateral creditor. China remains the biggest holder of Sri Lankan bilateral debt, and the relationship is shifting from mega-project lending toward trade, yuan-denominated credit, grants and investment in ports and energy.

Coverage also follows the wider competition around Colombo: India's growing security and infrastructure role, the Hambantota lease and its commercial revival, the stalled free trade agreement, and the question of whether Chinese lending is a development partnership or a debt trap.
