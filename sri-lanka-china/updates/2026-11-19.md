- **Sinopec's $3.7bn Hambantota refinery is being scrapped** — The Cabinet-appointed negotiating committee has recommended terminating the agreement and inviting fresh proposals, after Sinopec held out for 40% of output sold domestically, a 700-acre lease against the 500 acres first allocated, and decades of tax holidays. The Committee on Public Finance was told on 3 November that no final Cabinet decision had been taken, and Sinopec had already written in March or April 2026 asking to withdraw ([Sunday Times](https://www.sundaytimes.lk/261115/business-times/sinopec-refinery-deal-faces-fresh-investor-search-663512.html), [The Diplomat](https://thediplomat.com/2026/11/sinopec-demands-derail-oil-refinery-plans-at-sri-lankas-hambantota)).
- **The refinery loss is the second Chinese exit in a month and leaves the port without its industrial anchor** — It follows China Merchants Port Holdings' withdrawal from the $392m Colombo logistics hub, and with the IEA projecting only about 770,000 bpd of net global refining capacity added in 2027, almost all through expansions, any replacement bidder is likely to demand similar market access ([The Diplomat](https://thediplomat.com/2026/11/sinopec-demands-derail-oil-refinery-plans-at-sri-lankas-hambantota)).
- **Colombo's stated reason is energy sovereignty, not the investor's nationality** — The government changed position after private fuel suppliers cut imports during this year's oil shock and CPC had to step in, concluding that handing Sinopec roughly 40,000 bpd of a 120,000–140,000 bpd market would widen exposure to future price shocks ([The Diplomat](https://thediplomat.com/2026/11/sinopec-demands-derail-oil-refinery-plans-at-sri-lankas-hambantota)).
- **The 2027 budget puts a China FTA and RCEP back into stated policy** — Presenting the budget on 12 November, President Dissanayake said the government is exploring a new FTA with China and has applied to join RCEP, alongside a committee to expand the India FTA and planned CEPAs with the UAE ([Newswire](https://www.newswire.lk/2026/11/13/sri-lanka-appoints-committee-to-expand-free-trade-agreement-with-india)).
- **The CCP is deepening the party channel to the JVP as the investment channel narrows** — Tilvin Silva's delegation met Politburo member Shi Taifeng and International Department minister Liu Jianchao, who reportedly said no one would be allowed to undermine CCP–JVP ties, and a further CCP delegation is expected in Colombo on party organisation and discipline ([ISDP](https://www.isdp.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Brief-Tabita-Nov-17-2026.pdf)).
