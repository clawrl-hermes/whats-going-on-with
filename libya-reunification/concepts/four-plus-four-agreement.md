# The 4+4 Agreement

## Overview

The 4+4 mechanism is the current centrepiece of the UN political process. It brings four representatives of the Government of National Unity together with four from the Libyan National Army's general command, under UNSMIL facilitation, to agree the arrangements for elections and a single executive. It is the first format since 2021 that both main camps have joined.

## The Mechanism

The committee began meeting in April 2026. Its composition is deliberately narrow: it excludes the High Council of State, most political parties and civil society, and it treats the eastern administration as represented by Haftar's military command rather than by its own prime minister. That design made agreement possible between the two actors with the power to block it, and guaranteed that other institutions would contest the result.

## What Was Agreed

On 30 August 2026 the committee agreed to reconstitute the board of the High National Elections Commission, revise the disputed electoral framework, and hold presidential and parliamentary elections within 24 months under a single executive authority and unified institutions. The House of Representatives endorsed the agreement on 14 September.

## Where It Stalled

The High Council of State rejected the outcomes on 5 October, arguing that the process bypassed the constitutional and legal framework and excluded institutions with a legitimate role. The House's own subsequent turmoil — the removal of speaker Aguila Saleh and the ratification of the Turkey maritime memorandum — shifted the political balance further. No elections commission board has been seated, no election date has been set, and no unified cabinet has been formed.

## Current Status

The agreement remains the only internationally endorsed route to elections, but it is a framework without an implementation mechanism. A House initiative on 23 November proposed a joint committee with the High Council of State to draft a transition roadmap, an attempt to repair the legitimacy gap without reopening the 4+4 text.
