# Priority Sources

Curated outlets and institutions for Libya's reunification. Check Regional Specialists first, then Official Sources, then Think Tanks, then run the weekly Tavily queries.

## Regional Specialists

- **Libya Herald** — https://libyaherald.com
  English-language daily with the strongest business, oil and central bank coverage. Independent, but its reporting is friendlier to the eastern camp and it reproduces official statements at length.
- **The Libya Observer** — https://libyaobserver.ly
  Tripoli-based English outlet with fast coverage of the GNU, the High Council of State and western Libyan politics. Openly sympathetic to the western camp; treat its framing of eastern actors with care.
- **218 News** — https://218.tv
  Private Libyan television with breaking news and interviews across both camps. Arabic-only; verify claims against a second outlet.
- **Al-Wasat** — https://alwasat.ly
  Arabic news site with a Misrata perspective, useful for armed-group politics and western Libya that the English outlets miss.
- **Libya Al-Ahrar** — https://libyaalahrar.tv
  Arabic channel close to the Tripoli government; strong on GNU appointments and security developments, with a clear western bias.
- **LANA** — https://lana.gov.ly
  The Tripoli-based national news agency. Primary source for GNU and High Council of State statements; state-controlled, so it records positions rather than tests them.

## Think Tanks

- **Atlantic Council (MENASource)** — https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/blogs/menasource/
  Frequent, sourced analysis of Libyan politics and economics, including the maritime file and budget unification. Pro-Western Atlanticist framing.
- **ECFR** — https://ecfr.eu
  European analysis of Libya's elite bargains and migration file. Useful counterweight to US-centric commentary; European policy perspective.
- **Carnegie Middle East Center** — https://carnegie-mec.org
  Long-form political economy and armed-group analysis. Slower than the news, but reliable on institutional dynamics.
- **Security Council Report** — https://www.securitycouncilreport.org
  Tracks UNSMIL mandate negotiations, sanctions and Council divisions. Primary-quality reporting on the diplomatic track.
- **African Security Analysis** — https://www.africansecurityanalysis.com
  Short, judgment-led pieces on Libyan security and the UN process. Independent; useful for implementation risk.
- **Middle East Institute** — https://www.mei.edu
  Analysis of Libya's governance, economy and external patrons. Washington-based; sometimes mirrors US policy assumptions.
- **Chatham House** — https://www.chathamhouse.org
  Strong on Libyan political economy and the central bank. Cloudflare blocks automated extraction; use a site search fallback.
- **International Crisis Group** — https://www.crisisgroup.org
  Conflict analysis and actor mapping. Cloudflare and paywall issues; use a site search fallback and cite the published report page.

## Official Sources

- **UNSMIL** — https://unsmil.unmissions.org
  Primary source for the roadmap, the 4+4 process and Tetteh's statements. UN framing; read alongside Libyan reactions.
- **UN News — Libya** — https://news.un.org/en/focus/libya
  Summaries of Security Council meetings and humanitarian updates.
- **US State Department — Libya** — https://www.state.gov/countries-areas/libya/
  Statements, sanctions designations and the official US position on the Boulos track.
- **Turkish Ministry of Foreign Affairs** — https://www.mfa.gov.tr
  Turkish statements on Libya, the maritime memorandum and military cooperation.
- **Egyptian Ministry of Foreign Affairs** — https://www.mfa.gov.eg
  Cairo's position on Libya, the border, and its contacts with both camps.
- **Government of National Unity** — https://pm.gov.ly
  Dbeibah's office: cabinet decisions, budget statements and official visits.
- **Central Bank of Libya** — https://cbl.gov.ly/en
  Governor statements, board meetings, exchange-rate policy and the bank's account of the succession dispute.
- **National Oil Corporation** — https://noc.ly
  Production figures, force majeure notices and pipeline disruption statements.

## Advocacy & Diaspora

- **Human Rights Watch — Libya** — https://www.hrw.org/middle-east/n-africa/libya
  Documentation of detention, migration and armed-group abuses. Advocacy framing; useful for accountability questions the political tracks avoid.
- **Amnesty International — Libya** — https://www.amnesty.org/en/location/middle-east-and-north-africa/libya/
  Rights reporting and campaign statements.
- **Libya Analysis** — https://www.libya-analysis.com
  Independent commentary and analysis from Libyan researchers; diaspora perspective, variable frequency.

## Selection Guidance

Check in this order: Regional Specialists (especially Libya Herald and The Libya Observer), then Official Sources for positions and documents, then Think Tanks for analysis, then the weekly Tavily queries for anything missed. When the camps report the same event differently, keep both versions in `sources.md` only if each adds a distinct claim; otherwise cite the primary statement and one independent report.
