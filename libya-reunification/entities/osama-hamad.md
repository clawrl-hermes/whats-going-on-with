# Osama Hamad

Hamad heads the Government of National Stability, the Benghazi-based administration appointed by the House of Representatives in 2022. His government runs eastern Libya and most southern cities, pays salaries and provides services, but it has no international recognition and no seat at the 4+4 table, where the east is represented by Haftar's military command instead.

## Key Actions

- **May 2023:** Replaced Fathi Bashagha as prime minister of the eastern administration after Bashagha's failed attempt to enter Tripoli.
- **2024-2026:** Administered the eastern budget and public payroll while the House of Representatives refused to recognise the Tripoli government.
- **October 2026:** Issued a decree barring Iranian and Afghan nationals from entering territory under eastern control, extending earlier nationality-based restrictions.
- **November 2026:** Remained outside the US and UN mediation tracks as the 4+4 process and the Boulos plan negotiated directly with Haftar's camp.

## Current Position

Hamad is the formal head of a government that is being negotiated around. The 4+4 mechanism pairs the Tripoli government with the LNA's general command, and the US plan allocates the presidency to Saddam Haftar and the premiership to Dbeibah, leaving no obvious role for the eastern prime minister. His administration's functions would have to be absorbed by any unified executive, which gives him leverage over implementation even without a seat at the table.

## Constraints

- His government lacks international recognition and cannot borrow or sign enforceable agreements abroad.
- He depends on the House of Representatives and Haftar for funding and armed support.
- The east's oil revenue flows through the Tripoli-based Central Bank, leaving him dependent on a rival institution.
