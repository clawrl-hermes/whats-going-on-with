# Khalifa Haftar

Haftar built the Libyan National Army into the east's dominant armed force and the military backbone of the Benghazi-based Government of National Stability. He is not a head of state, but no unified government can be formed without his consent. His foreign backers — Egypt, the UAE and Russia — supply the weapons, money and trainers that make his forces the strongest single military actor in Libya.

## Key Actions

- **2014:** Launched Operation Dignity against Islamist groups in Benghazi, becoming the east's paramount commander.
- **April 2019:** Ordered an offensive on Tripoli that failed after 14 months, entrenching the western camp and Turkish intervention.
- **August 2026:** Backed the 4+4 agreement through the LNA delegation, accepting a single executive and elections within 24 months.
- **October 2026:** Allowed the removal of speaker Aguila Saleh and the ratification of the Turkey maritime memorandum, shifting the east's diplomatic alignment.
- **November 2026:** Sent his son Khaled to Paris for military unification talks with the western chief of staff.

## Current Position

Haftar controls the east and much of the south, but his army is a coalition of units and foreign-supported formations rather than a national institution. He has moved from rejecting the political process to shaping it, and the US plan would give his family the presidency of a unified state while leaving the premiership to Dbeibah. That arrangement is his best route to legitimacy short of elections, and the reason he has not blocked it.

## Constraints

- His health and age make succession the central question; Saddam's political rise is not yet accepted across the LNA.
- His forces depend on Russian, Egyptian and Emirati support that a unified government would have to renegotiate.
- Western Libyan armed groups and Turkey would resist any arrangement that puts him or his family in command.
