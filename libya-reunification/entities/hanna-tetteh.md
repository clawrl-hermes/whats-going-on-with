# Hanna Tetteh

Tetteh heads the UN Support Mission in Libya and is the principal international broker of the reunification process. She took over a mission with a weak mandate and a country whose main actors each maintain armed forces and separate budgets. Her strategy has been to build a route to elections through a structured dialogue rather than to impose a settlement.

## Key Actions

- **August 2025:** Presented the three-pillar roadmap to the Security Council: an electoral framework, a unified government, and a structured dialogue.
- **August 2026:** Facilitated the Smaller Convening, the 4+4 talks that produced the 30 August agreement on the elections commission and a 24-month timetable.
- **October 2026:** Briefed the Security Council before its unanimous renewal of UNSMIL's mandate until 31 October 2027.
- **November 2026:** Visited Benghazi and met the new House speaker, then used the Mediterranean Dialogues in Naples to press for coordinated international support.

## Current Position

Tetteh has kept a process alive that most Libyan actors do not want to lose, but she cannot compel the High Council of State to accept the 4+4 outcomes or force the US and Turkish tracks to subordinate themselves to the UN. Her influence depends on the Security Council's unanimity and on the parties' need for international legitimacy, both of which are weakening as bilateral deals multiply.

## Constraints

- UNSMIL has no enforcement mechanism and no peacekeepers.
- The High Council of State rejects the procedural basis of the agreement she facilitated.
- The US and Turkish mediation tracks can deliver what the UN cannot, and can bypass her process.
