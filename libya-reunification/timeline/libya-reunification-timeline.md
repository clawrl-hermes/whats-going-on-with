# Timeline: Libya's Reunification

## 2026
- **November:**
  - Rival chiefs of staff meet in Paris on military unification and border control.
  - US envoy Massad Boulos pushes a plan keeping Dbeibah as prime minister and making Saddam Haftar head of the Presidential Council.
  - Central Bank succession dispute and a sliding dinar trigger warnings of an economic crisis.
  - Pipeline closures cut about 365,000 barrels a day of oil output.
  - House of Representatives proposes a joint committee with the High Council of State to draft a transition roadmap.
- **October:**
  - House of Representatives removes speaker Aguila Saleh and elects al-Salhin Abdelnabi.
  - Eastern parliament ratifies the 2019 Turkey-Libya maritime memorandum.
  - Security Council renews UNSMIL's mandate until 31 October 2027.
- **September:**
  - House of Representatives endorses the 4+4 agreement; High Council of State later rejects it.
- **August:**
  - UN-facilitated 4+4 committee agrees to reconstitute the elections commission and hold elections within 24 months under a single executive.
- **April:**
  - Rival authorities agree a unified budget framework for the first time in 13 years.

## 2025
- **August:**
  - UN envoy Hanna Tetteh presents a three-pillar political roadmap to the Security Council.

## 2023
- **May:**
  - Osama Hamad replaces Fathi Bashagha as prime minister of the eastern Government of National Stability.

## 2022
- **February:**
  - House of Representatives selects Fathi Bashagha as prime minister; Dbeibah refuses to hand over.
- **August:**
  - Clashes in Tripoli end the Bashagha government's attempt to enter the capital.

## 2021
- **February:**
  - Libyan Political Dialogue Forum selects Dbeibah to lead a Government of National Unity.
- **December:**
  - Presidential election is postponed indefinitely.

## 2020
- **October:**
  - Rival military commissions sign a nationwide ceasefire and a prisoner-exchange agreement in Geneva.

## 2019
- **April:**
  - Khalifa Haftar launches an offensive to capture Tripoli; it stalls on the city's outskirts.

## 2016
- **March:**
  - The UN-backed Government of National Accord arrives in Tripoli without control of the east.

## 2015
- **December:**
  - Rival parliaments sign the Skhirat agreement, creating a Presidential Council and a unity government.

## 2014
- **May:**
  - Haftar launches Operation Dignity against Islamist groups in Benghazi.
- **June:**
  - Parliamentary elections produce a House of Representatives that moves to Tobruk.
- **August:**
  - A rival government and parliament take control of Tripoli.

## 2011
- **October:**
  - Muammar Gaddafi is killed and the National Transitional Council declares liberation.
