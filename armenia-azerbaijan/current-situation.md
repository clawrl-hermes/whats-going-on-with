Armenia and Azerbaijan have still not signed the peace treaty, and Baku's stated precondition has not moved: the text is agreed, but Aliyev will not sign before Armenia removes the constitutional reference to Karabakh, with presidential aide Hikmet Hajiyev adding a demand for an apology on 26 October. Pashinyan and Aliyev met in Dublin on 12 November and their readouts covered TRIPP planning, trade, border delimitation and new working groups on shared rivers, but not the constitution. Four days earlier the CSTO had stripped Armenia of its voting rights and its right to appoint senior officials, so Yerevan now sits inside the EAEU while its European track produced customs and tax programme agreements with Brussels on 12 November. TRIPP ratification is complete and design work is running ahead of the development company, while the Turkish border at Margara remains shut. The binding constraint is arithmetic: the constitutional draft has been ready since March and must clear the Constitutional Reform Council by 30 December for a 2027 referendum, but Civil Contract lost the two-thirds majority in June.

## The Open Question

Will December settle the price of Armenia's pivot, as Pashinyan meets Moscow three times and the constitutional draft reaches the Reform Council, or will both files simply slip into 2027?
