The peace treaty is agreed in text but held hostage to Armenia's constitution. Baku will not sign until Yerevan removes the Karabakh reference, and Pashinyan cannot amend the constitution without the two-thirds majority he lost in June, so the file is now a domestic political problem on both sides. The practical settlement is advancing anyway, through trade, TRIPP and border work, while Russia has shifted from coercion to bargaining and the EU has opened a trade track. The binding constraint is arithmetic and timing: the draft must clear the Reform Council by 30 December for a 2027 referendum.

## The Open Question

Can Pashinyan deliver the constitutional change Baku demands without the parliamentary majority needed to pass it?
