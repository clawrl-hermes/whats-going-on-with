# Ararat Mirzoyan

Mirzoyan has run Armenian diplomacy through the peace process, the TRIPP negotiations and the break with Moscow. He is the official who sets the timetable in public, and his statements are usually a test of where the government is prepared to land.

## Key Actions
- **July 2026:** Presents the TRIPP framework to parliament and the Constitutional Court, including the 74/26 split and 49-year lease.
- **September 2026:** Meets Bayramov at the UN General Assembly and agrees to promote a "positive agenda" in multilateral forums, even as a heritage dispute escalates.
- **October 2026:** Tells parliament Iran is "satisfied" with TRIPP after his Tehran visit, and says Armenia is ready to sign a peace treaty by end-2026 or in 2027.
- **October 2026:** Rules out returning to the CSTO, days before Moscow moves to suspend Armenia's voting rights.
- **November 2026:** Says the Turkish border "could open at any time" and that Margara is technically ready.

## Current Position
Mirzoyan puts the treaty earlier than Pashinyan does — end-2026 or 2027 against the prime minister's 2031 — while conceding that Baku will not sign before constitutional change. He argues Baku does not formally table the constitution as a negotiating demand, but treats the issue as the practical precondition. On Europe he is cautious: Armenia values EU engagement but has no membership timetable.

## Constraints
- He negotiates with a narrower mandate than Pashinyan's, and any concession is read at home as surrender.
- The foreign ministry cannot deliver the constitutional vote, which is a parliamentary and referendum problem.
