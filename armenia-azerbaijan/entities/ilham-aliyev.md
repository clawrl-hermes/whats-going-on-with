# Ilham Aliyev

Aliyev has ruled Azerbaijan since 2003 and holds the strongest hand in the peace process: his army won the 2020 war, retook Nagorno-Karabakh in 2023, and occupies strategic positions inside Armenia's internationally recognised border.

## Key Actions
- **September 2023:** A 24-hour military operation dissolves the self-declared Nagorno-Karabakh republic; over 100,000 Armenians flee.
- **August 2025:** He accepts the US-brokered peace framework at the White House summit with Trump and Pashinyan.
- **June 2026:** After his re-election, he adds demands beyond constitutional change — enclaves, border adjustments and resettlement of Azerbaijanis displaced from Armenia.
- **October 2026:** He tells his party congress that Armenia's "territorial demands" remain the obstacle, while deliberately dialling down rhetoric so as not to complicate Pashinyan's referendum.

## Current Position
Baku runs a two-track posture: it keeps the de facto peace and the trade that has resumed, but withholds signature until Armenia amends its constitution. Aliyev has little appetite for renewed war now that the territories are reclaimed, and his focus has shifted to the Middle Corridor and Central Asia. The "Western Azerbaijan" campaign remains state-directed and is used as a mirror demand for any return of Armenians.

## Constraints
- Only about 40,000 of a planned 140,000 Azerbaijanis have resettled in Karabakh, weakening the practical basis for the return demand.
- Defence takes 20.8% of the budget even as Baku says there is "no war".
- The public rift with Turkey and US congressional bills (H.R. 9087) complicate his diplomatic position.
