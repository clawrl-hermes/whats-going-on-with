# Recep Tayyip Erdoğan

## Relevance to the Topic
Turkey is Azerbaijan's closest strategic ally and was decisive in the 2020 war. It has kept its border with Armenia closed since 1993, making normalization the last piece of the regional settlement and a direct incentive for Yerevan.

## Key Actions
- **May 2026:** Ankara lifts restrictions on direct trade declarations with Armenia after 33 years, and the Akhalkalaki-Kars railway reopens for Armenian freight.
- **June 2026:** A first Armenian-Turkish business forum is held in Kars.
- **September 2026:** Erdoğan meets Pashinyan for the first time at the UN General Assembly.
- **November 2026:** He visits Baku to repair the sharpest public rift with Azerbaijan in years, after disputes over the school hijab ban and Israel.

## Current Position
The border remains closed, but the Margara crossing is renovated and technically ready; Mirzoyan says it "could open at any time". Ankara still formally ties full normalization to an Armenia-Azerbaijan peace treaty, and goods continue to move through Georgia. Analysts are sceptical of an early opening: Turkish policy is a rational calculation that keeps Baku on side, and the BTK railway through Georgia is already underused.

## Constraints
- Coordination with Baku limits how far Ankara can move before a treaty.
- The economic case for a new route is weak while existing rail capacity is idle.
- Opening the border would not by itself amount to full normalization.
