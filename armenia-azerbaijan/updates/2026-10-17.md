- **Pashinyan sets a 2031 deadline for peace** — He aims to sign and ratify a treaty with Azerbaijan before his term ends, and proposed a mutual enclave/exclave exchange (Karki, Barkhudarlyu and Yukhari Askipara for Artsvashen) to clear the last territorial obstacle.
- **Russia and Armenia open talks on the railway concession** — A three-month working group will examine Russian investment in South Caucasus Railway; Moscow is resisting transferring the concession while declining to fund it, and Pashinyan says $3B would end the political debate.
- **EU trade measures take effect** — Armenian exporters shifted sharply toward the EU and Central Asia: fruit exports that went 87% to Russia a year earlier were split between Uzbekistan (48%) and Georgia (34%), with EU-bound exports up 80% to $516M in the first half of 2026.
- **The civil service turns west** — A talent programme drew 1,141 applicants from top-200 universities and placed Harvard- and Erasmus-trained deputies in the health and economy ministries, rerouting recruitment from Russian to Western institutions.
- **Turkey and Azerbaijan drift apart in public** — Herzog's CICA visit to Baku, flown on an Azerbaijani jet around Turkey's airspace ban and met by a Turkish walk-out, added to the hijab-ban dispute and Azerbaijan's new Israeli rifle factory.
