- **TRIPP becomes law in Armenia** — Parliament ratified the Armenia-US framework agreement 63–32 on 28 October, creating a development company that is 74% US-owned for an initial 49 years. Only the ruling Civil Contract faction voted in favour. ([JAMnews](https://jam-news.net/armenian-parliament-ratifies-framework-agreement-on-trump-route-tripp-project))
- **A trilateral structure finally exists** — On 6 November Washington, Yerevan and Baku announced two technical planning groups on transport and electricity transmission, the first formal three-way working mechanism since the August 2025 Washington declaration. ([Trend](https://www.trend.az/azerbaijan/4243996.html))
- **Mirzoyan moves the goalposts forward** — The foreign minister said on 28 October that Armenia is ready to sign by end-2026 or in 2027, far earlier than Pashinyan's 2031 target, while confirming Baku will not sign before constitutional change. ([Hetq](https://hetq.am/en/article/185073))
- **Pashinyan picks his constitutional fight** — His government detained Samvel Karapetyan and 35 associates over alleged damage at Electric Networks of Armenia, and the opposition answered with a "pan-national consolidation" against "Aliyev's Constitution". Every major opposition leader is now in pretrial detention. ([OC Media](https://oc-media.org/armenian-opposition-forms-alliance-against-plan-to-amend-constitution-following-karapetyan-arrest/))
- **The Constitutional Court is now fully Pashinyan's** — Former Civil Contract MP Vladimir Vardanyan was elected its president on 6 November, just as the court prepares to review the amendments Baku demands. ([OC Media](https://oc-media.org/former-armenian-ruling-party-mp-elected-president-of-constitutional-court/))
- **Russia's alliance is formally abandoned, its market is not** — Pashinyan said Armenia's CSTO interests are "exhausted" and Mirzoyan ruled out returning, days before a Moscow session on suspending Armenia's voting rights — but Armenia stays in the EAEU and now targets an EU application in 2027–2028. ([MassisPost](https://massispost.com/2026/11/pashinyan-armenia-not-planning-to-leave-eurasian-economic-union-says-ties-with-csto-have-run-their-course), [OC Media](https://oc-media.org/pashinyan-says-armenia-could-apply-for-eu-membership-next-year/))
- **The Turkey border is close and not close** — Mirzoyan says it could open "at any time" with Margara ready; Alexander Iskandaryan puts the odds "quite low", arguing the Ankara-Baku rift will not change Turkish policy. ([Turkish Minute](https://www.turkishminute.com/2026/11/05/armenia-turkey-border-could-open-at-any-time-minister-says), [JAMnews](https://jam-news.net/will-turkey-open-its-border-with-armenia-amid-tensions-with-baku-views-from-yerevan/))
