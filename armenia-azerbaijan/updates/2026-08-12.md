- **The Washington summit anniversary is marked** — A joint US-Armenia-Azerbaijan statement lists eight benchmarks including TRIPP surveys, the $402M fund and the OSCE Minsk Group's dissolution; the treaty remains unsigned.
- **TRIPP+ funding doubles to $402M** — The US doubles the enterprise fund, with Armenia the primary beneficiary through 2036.
- **Russia's trade war widens** — Moscow bans 70,000 bottles of Armenian water and soft drinks and warns tourists; Pashinyan says the restrictions could become an EAEU problem.
- **Azerbaijan confirms a major arms package** — 40 JF-17C Block III fighters from China and Pakistan, plus Chinese and Turkish air-defence systems.
- **Connectivity advances on several fronts** — Azerbaijan and Georgia fast-track Middle Corridor freight, Armenia and Iran plan a second Araks bridge, and a Turkish op-ed calls for full diplomatic relations.
