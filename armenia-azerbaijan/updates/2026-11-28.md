- **TRIPP leaves the drawing board in Syunik** — Pashinyan and Trump announced the practical phase on 23 November, with machinery levelling ground in Meghri and the TRIPP Development Company launched as the project vehicle; Deputy Foreign Minister Vahan Kostanyan said the rail route is fixed and construction starts in 2027 once the design study reports early next year ([Caucasus Watch](https://caucasuswatch.de/en/news/armenia-and-us-launch-practical-phase-of-tripp.html), [Anadolu Agency](https://aa.com.tr/en/americas/trump-pashinyan-announce-launch-of-tripp-corridor-project-in-armenia/4134536), [JAMnews](https://jam-news.net/construction-of-railway-under-trump-route-tripp-project-to-begin-in-2027/)).
- **Pashinyan publishes an 89-article constitution that drops the Karabakh claim** — The 23 November draft removes the preamble reference to the Declaration of Independence and its reunification clause, calls Armenia a "secular" state for the first time, and lowers the threshold for calling a referendum from two-thirds to three-fifths, which would let Civil Contract call one without opposition votes ([OC Media](https://oc-media.org/explainer-whats-different-in-armenias-proposed-new-constitution/)).
- **Baku says the draft clears its precondition** — Bayramov, briefing alongside Italy's Antonio Tajani in Baku, said the only Azerbaijani interest is that the new constitution contain no territorial claims, that "judging by everything" it does not, and that "no additional obstacles will remain" to signing the treaty once the issue is settled ([Report.az](https://report.az/en/other/bayramov-baku-positively-evaluates-absence-of-territorial-claims-in-armenia-s-draft-constitution)).
- **Pashinyan moves to end the Russian border-guard presence** — He said on 26 November that Armenia will take full responsibility for its borders in the coming years and thanked the FSB troops who still patrol sections of the Turkish and Iranian frontiers, without setting a timeline ([Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/armenia-plans-full-withdrawal-of-russian-border-guards-in-coming-years-prime-minister-says)).
- **The Kremlin names its price for 3 December** — Peskov said the Moscow talks will turn on "the choice the Armenian side is making", called EU integration incompatible with EAEU membership, and confirmed the future of the Gyumri base will be raised; Pashinyan said the same week that tensions with Russia were falling, not rising ([The Armenian Report](https://www.thearmenianreport.com/post/russia-to-ask-pashinyan-to-clarify-armenia-s-eu-ambitions-and-future-of-russian-military-base-at-dec)).
- **The Peace Bridge track restarts at its largest size** — Sixty Azerbaijani and Armenian civil-society delegates met in Dilijan on 26–27 November, the biggest round since the platform began, and formalised six committees; Security Council Secretary Simonyan and Deputy Foreign Minister Kostanyan addressed the sessions ([Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/11/27/azerbaijani-and-armenian-civil-societies-seek-new-history-in-new-round-of-peace-talks)).
