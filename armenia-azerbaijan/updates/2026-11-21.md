- **The draft constitution lands on 23 November** — Pashinyan said on 20 November that the Justice Ministry will publish the text on Monday and that he will address the nation, opening the public consultation and Venice Commission clock Baku has waited on; Justice Minister Srbuhi Galyan said ten days earlier that more than 90% of the draft had cleared the Constitutional Reform Council ([NEWS.am](https://news.am/en/news/1076119)).
- **Pashinyan puts his premiership on the referendum** — In parliament on 18 November he said there are "guarantees" the treaty will be signed and ratified once a new constitution is adopted, promised to resign if voters reject it and not to seek the premiership again if they approve, and asked the opposition for the six votes that would take the ruling party from 64 to the 70 needed to call the vote ([Eurasianet](https://eurasianet.org/pashinyan-pushes-for-constitutional-referendum), [ARMENPRESS](https://armenpress.am/en/article/1266069)).
- **TRIPP enters delivery, and Baku claims a seat in the design** — On 19 November AECOM briefed Armenian officials on preliminary findings of the TRIPP railway engineering study and discussed financing, a day after Bayramov said Baku must have a voice in the design because the line's entry and exit points sit on the shared border; Pashinyan separately said the southern border section will be delimited precisely where the railway, pipeline and highway are to run ([Eurasianet](https://eurasianet.org/pashinyan-pushes-for-constitutional-referendum), [Trend](https://www.trend.az/azerbaijan/4248003.html)).
- **Turkish Cargo opens a scheduled Istanbul–Yerevan freighter** — The first scheduled cargo flight ran on 15 November, days after Pashinyan met Erdoğan in Antalya at the COP31 leaders' summit, extending a transport relationship that still has no land border under it; Ankara continues to tie reopening to the peace treaty ([OC Media](https://oc-media.org/turkey-launches-regular-cargo-flights-to-armenia-as-relations-improve)).
- **Yerevan changes ambassadors in Moscow before three December visits** — President Khachaturyan appointed Samvel Mkrtchyan, a former envoy to Poland and Lebanon and ex-representative to NATO, on 20 November and recalled Gurgen Arsenyan, giving Pashinyan a fresh channel into the Kremlin for the 3, 11 and 22 December rounds ([Caucasian Knot](https://www.eng.kavkaz-uzel.eu/articles/80385)).
- **Berlin adopts Baku's sequencing in public** — German Foreign Minister Johann Wadephul, back from visits to Baku and Yerevan, said on 21 November that before the treaty is signed "Armenia still needs to amend its constitution or adopt a new one", putting a European government on record behind the precondition Yerevan says Azerbaijan never formally tables at the negotiating table ([Report.az](https://report.az/en/region/german-foreign-minister-constitutional-change-in-armenia-necessary-to-finalize-peace-deal-with-azerbaijan), [OC Media](https://oc-media.org/aliyev-meets-german-foreign-minister-as-protest-over-azerbaijani-prisoners-continues-in-berlin)).
