- **Carnegie proposes a post-election peace roadmap** — Argues Pashinyan's narrower mandate makes a visible peace dividend urgent, and proposes a Trump-Pashinyan-Aliyev meeting at the July NATO summit plus opening the Turkey border for direct trade.
- **The US DFC publishes the TRIPP implementation structure** — A 74/26 US-Armenia joint venture with a multimodal scope and World Bank projections of ~$100M in annual transport savings, but no DFC funding committed.
- **Aliyev escalates his demands at the UN** — Return of former Azerbaijani enclaves, territorial adjustments through delimitation, and resettlement of 300,000 Azerbaijanis in Armenia.
- **Armenia sets a 2030 border-reopening target** — Deputy FM Kostanyan said full reopening with Turkey and Azerbaijan is targeted for 2030, with some routes operational earlier.
- **Connectivity corridors are framed as a geopolitical choice** — The German Marshall Fund argues Crossroads of Peace, the Middle Corridor and TRIPP are converging into a Euro-Atlantic route-diversification effort.
