- **Leaders meet in Dublin with the treaty still unsigned** — Pashinyan and Aliyev met on 12 November on the sidelines of the European Political Community summit and reviewed TRIPP planning, trade, border delimitation and confidence-building measures, and announced working groups on transboundary rivers. Both readouts cite 105,000 tonnes of cargo forwarded to Armenia through Azerbaijan since November 2025 and the coming electricity-grid interconnection, and neither mentions the constitution ([OC Media](https://oc-media.org/pashinyan-and-aliyev-meet-in-dublin-to-discuss-trump-route-trade-and-water-cooperation/), [Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/eurasia/aliyev-pashinyan-discuss-advancing-azerbaijan-armenia-peace-process-in-dublin/4123428)).
- **CSTO turns Armenia's frozen membership into a formal penalty** — On 11 November the bloc stripped Armenia of its voting rights and its right to appoint representatives to senior posts under Article 25 of its charter, which Lavrov attributed to contributions more than two years in arrears while insisting Moscow is "not slamming the door shut". Yerevan keeps EAEU membership and is widening its EU track in parallel ([OC Media](https://oc-media.org/armenia-stripped-of-voting-rights-in-russia-led-csto-as-moscow-relations-continue-to-deteriorate/)).
- **TRIPP ratification is complete while design work runs ahead of the company** — President Vahagn Khachaturyan signed the ratification law on 10 November, finishing the process begun with the 63-32 vote on 28 October in which only the ruling faction voted in favour. Pashinyan told MPs on 2 November that Armenia would commission the design work itself and hand the results to TRIPP Development once it exists ([Caucasian Knot](https://www.eng.kavkaz-uzel.eu/articles/80155), [Eurasianet](https://eurasianet.org/armenia-picking-up-pace-on-tripp-work)).
- **Armenia attaches its customs system to the EU as Russian restrictions bite** — Yerevan signed agreements on 12 November joining the EU's Customs and Fiscalis programmes, with an 80% reduction on its operational contribution, weeks after the EU Council suspended tariffs on roughly 80% of Armenian exports. The tax and customs track is now the most concrete piece of the European relationship ([OC Media](https://oc-media.org/armenia-signs-agreements-to-strengthen-tax-cooperation-with-eu-amid-russian-trade-restrictions/)).
- **Baku jails two more journalists as the crackdown continues** — The Baku Grave Crimes Court sentenced Meclis.info head Imran Aliyev to 10 years and activist Elgiz Gahraman to nine years on money-laundering, smuggling and tax charges on 12 November, with a third defendant receiving a suspended term. Aliyev links his case to his platform's monitoring of parliament ([OC Media](https://oc-media.org/two-more-azerbaijani-journalists-receive-long-prison-sentences-as-crackdown-continues/)).
- **Pashinyan books three Moscow visits for December** — Speaking in Dublin, he said he will travel to Russia on 3, 11 and 22 December, presenting the trips as the mirror of three EU visits in November and denying any intent to damage relations with Moscow. The schedule puts the Russian file on dated deadlines rather than leaving it to the CSTO machinery ([ARMENPRESS](https://armenpress.am/en/article/1265662/amp)).
