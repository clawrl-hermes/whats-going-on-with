# Timeline: Armenia-Azerbaijan Relations

## 2020
- **September:**
  - Second Nagorno-Karabakh War begins. Azerbaijan, backed by Turkey, launches a major offensive against Armenian forces in the disputed territory.
- **November:**
  - Russia-brokered ceasefire ends the 44-day war. Azerbaijan regains significant territory. Russian peacekeepers deployed to Nagorno-Karabakh.

## 2021
- **April:**
  - Tensions rise as Azerbaijan detains Armenian soldiers near the border, violating the ceasefire.
- **November:**
  - Clashes along the Armenia-Azerbaijan border kill dozens on both sides.

## 2022
- **September:**
  - Azerbaijan launches a major offensive along the Armenia-Azerbaijan border, seizing strategic positions inside internationally recognized Armenian territory.
- **October:**
  - Pashinyan signals willingness to recognize Nagorno-Karabakh as part of Azerbaijan in exchange for peace guarantees.

## 2023
- **September:**
  - Azerbaijan launches a 24-hour military operation in Nagorno-Karabakh, dissolving the self-declared republic. Over 100,000 ethnic Armenians flee the region.
- **December:**
  - Armenia and Azerbaijan agree to exchange prisoners and begin peace talks.

## 2024
- **May:**
  - Armenia returns four border villages to Azerbaijan as a goodwill gesture.
- **July:**
  - Turkey and Armenia resume diplomatic normalization talks.
- **August:**
  - US-brokered negotiations on the TRUMP Route for International Peace and Prosperity (TRIPP) corridor begin.

## 2025
- **August:**
  - Trump brokers the Armenia-Azerbaijan peace framework at a White House summit. Both sides agree to pursue a comprehensive peace treaty.

## 2026
- **May:**
  - The Akhalkalaki-Kars railway reopens for Armenian freight.
- **June:**
  - Pashinyan's Civil Contract party wins Armenian parliamentary elections.
  - Aliyev escalates his preconditions, demanding the return of former Azerbaijani enclaves and the resettlement of 300,000 Azerbaijanis in Armenia.
- **July:**
  - Armenian government approves the TRIPP framework bill and sends it to the Constitutional Court.
- **August:**
  - Pashinyan and Aliyev meet in Bishkek.
- **September:**
  - Armenia ships its first exports to Azerbaijan.
- **October:**
  - Armenia's parliament ratifies the TRIPP framework agreement.
- **November:**
  - The US, Armenia and Azerbaijan launch two trilateral technical planning groups on transport and electricity transmission for TRIPP.
  - Armenian opposition parties form a "pan-national consolidation" against the draft constitution
