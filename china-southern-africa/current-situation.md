China's position in Southern Africa deepened in the past month even as Western counter-finance became more concrete. Chinese firms now hold 54% of the DRC's critical mineral projects, African processing capacity rose 28% in a year, and Zimbabwe's lithium earnings reached $2.16 billion in nine months. Botswana was elevated to a comprehensive strategic partnership during President Boko's visit, while the OECD documented Project Vault and Project Forge and the AFC named preferred bidders for Zambia's Lobito section. The contest has moved from mine ownership to processing and corridors, and the next test is whether Zimbabwe's 2027 export ban and the DRC's waiver regime force concentrate through plants that Chinese companies largely built.

## The Open Question

Can Western midstream and corridor money reach financial close before Zimbabwe's 2027 export ban and the DRC's waiver regime lock in Chinese processing?
