China's position in Southern Africa held through mid-November: its firms own 54% of the DRC's critical mineral projects, African processing capacity rose 28% in a year, and Zimbabwe's lithium earnings reached $2.16 billion in nine months. The first test of the beneficiation strategy has now come from the industry side, with Zimbabwe's miners asking Harare to move the January 2027 concentrate export ban to July 2027 because only one lithium salt plant is operating. Chinese capital is following the same logic, with investment in sub-Saharan Africa overtaking construction for the first time in 2025 and processing absorbing more of it than extraction. The contest has shifted from who owns the mines to who finishes the plants before the bans bite.

## The Open Question

Will Harare hold Zimbabwe's January 2027 concentrate ban, or grant the July delay its miners are asking for?
