China's position in Southern Africa is secure at the mine but less so at the plant. Chinese firms own over half the DRC's critical mineral projects and processing capacity is rising, yet Zimbabwe's miners want the January 2027 concentrate ban delayed because the sulphate plants are not ready. Chinese capital is following the same logic, moving from extraction into processing. The contest has shifted from who owns the ore to who finishes the plants before the bans bite.

## The Open Question

Can Zimbabwe and the DRC finish enough processing capacity to enforce their export bans without stranding production?
