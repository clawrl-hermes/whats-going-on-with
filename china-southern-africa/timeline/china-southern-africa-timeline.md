# Timeline: Chinese Infrastructure and Minerals in Southern Africa

## 2026
- **January:**
  - DH Mining opens a $200 million graphite plant at Nipepe, Mozambique.
- **April:**
  - Zimbabwe ships its first lithium sulphate from Huayou's Arcadia plant.
  - Chengxin and Sinomine receive the first lithium concentrate export quotas.
- **May:**
  - China grants zero-tariff treatment to 53 African countries.
  - AidData puts Chinese development finance to the DRC at $22 billion across 112 loans since 2000.
- **June:**
  - The EU announces a €12 billion investment package for South Africa, conditioned on beneficiation.
  - Kamoa-Kakula's copper smelter, Africa's largest, begins operating in the DRC.
  - Zimbabwe opens resource-backed infrastructure talks with China Railway.
  - The G7 adopts a critical minerals action plan.
- **July:**
  - Namibia signs eight agreements in Beijing covering uranium, lithium and rare-earth processing.
  - Mutapa confirms $300 million from Huayou and Tsingshan for the Sandawana lithium project.
  - Glencore sells 40% stakes in DRC copper-cobalt assets to a US-Gulf consortium.
  - Chinalco moves to acquire a 95% stake in Celsius Resources' Opuwo cobalt-copper project.
  - Zimbabwe designates 13 more minerals for a raw-export ban from 2027.
  - Angola secures $900 million of Chinese investment for the Barra do Dande port terminal.
  - South Africa opens its freight rail network to private operators.
  - Chinese BRI investment in Africa rises 254% to $33.5 billion in the first half of 2026.
- **August:**
  - South Africa pitches R2.2 trillion of energy investment to Chinese firms in Beijing.
  - A CRS report finds Africa supplied 99.9% of China's cobalt imports and 86% of its natural graphite in 2025.
  - A Chinese contractor wins a $2.7 billion railway project in Tanzania.
- **September:**
  - The DRC bans copper and cobalt concentrate exports, allowing only one-year strategic waivers.
  - Shenghe begins a heavy-minerals separation plant at Pangani, Tanzania.
  - Sinomine secures another 300,000-tonne Zimbabwean quota, taking announced Chinese investment to $2.8 billion.
  - China NERIN wins a $498 million copper tailings contract at Chingola.
  - Zimbabwe's nine-month lithium export earnings reach $2.16 billion.
- **October:**
  - Chinese firms hold stakes in 54% of the DRC's critical mining projects.
  - African mineral processing facilities rise 28% in a year, with Chinese groups involved in 15.
  - Botswana is elevated to a comprehensive strategic partnership during President Boko's Beijing visit.
- **November:**
  - China's BRI spending on metals and mining reaches a record $32 billion for 2025.
