- **Zimbabwe's lithium miners ask Harare to push the concentrate ban to July 2027** — A Chamber of Mines survey says the January 2027 deadline would strand 1.4 million tonnes of concentrate by end-2027, because only Huayou's plant is running: 33,807 tonnes of lithium sulphate shipped between April and September. The same survey projects sulphate output of 208,000 tonnes in 2027 once Sinomine and Sichuan Yahua plants start, against concentrate output of 2.3 million tonnes ([CNBC Africa](https://www.cnbcafrica.com/2026/zimbabwe-sees-2027-lithium-sulphate-output-at-208000-t-miners-seek-export-ban-delay)).
- **Zambia has drawn $12bn into mining since 2022, with Chinese processors the fastest-growing and least scrutinised tier** — President Hichilema put the figure at the Zambia Mining and Investment Forum on 19 October, naming CNMC among the leading investors alongside First Quantum, Barrick and Vedanta. Smaller Chinese-owned processing plants in the Copperbelt, some buying ore from artisanal miners, are expanding while safety and environmental oversight lags; the national geophysical survey is 98% complete ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/16/zambias-copper-boom-attracts-billions-in-investment-but-who-benefits)).
- **Chinese investment in sub-Saharan Africa overtook construction for the first time** — AEI's tracker puts 2025 investment at $10.4bn against $7.3bn of construction, down from a 2015 construction peak of $20.9bn. Metals took 75% of investment in 2023–25; Botswana and Tanzania entered the top five recipients, Tanzania on TAZARA-linked spending ([AEI](https://www.aei.org/research-products/report/china-in-sub-saharan-africa-some-facts-about-investing-and-building)).
- **Processing now absorbs more Chinese capital than extraction in Africa** — Rhodium Group figures cited by the Financial Times put manufacturing and processing investment at about $7.7bn in 2025, above the $4.8bn for resource extraction, reversing the pattern that built the infrastructure-for-resources model ([Xinde Marine News](https://www.xindemarinenews.com/news/2120682136710479874)).
