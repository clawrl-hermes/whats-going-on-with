- **CRS quantifies Africa's role in China's supply chain** — Africa supplied 99.9% of Chinese cobalt imports, 91% of chromium and 86% of natural graphite in 2025.
- **Chinese lending underpins major acquisitions** — BYD secured six African lithium mines through 2032, and state banks financed most of CMOC's Tenke purchase.
- **Export controls put downstream production at risk** — The IEA estimates $6.5 trillion of output depends on Chinese processing.
- **US-DRC partnership funds corridor work** — The 2025 agreement underpins a $553 million DFC loan for Lobito and $1 billion for DRC rail.
