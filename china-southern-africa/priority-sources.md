# Priority Sources — Chinese Infrastructure and Minerals in Southern Africa

These sources should be checked first during every source monitor run, before conducting broader Tavily searches. They are organized by category reflecting source value and trust level.

Compiled 2026-11-11 from the topic's existing source base and the high-signal source types in `domain-notes.md`. Sites marked "bot-blocked" or "paywalled" often still work through search; if extraction fails, search the outlet by name.

---

## Regional Specialists (high trust)

These outlets provide dedicated coverage of Southern Africa. Check each for new articles.

- **The Africa Report** — https://www.theafricareport.com
  Pan-African politics and business publication with the strongest single-outlet coverage of China-Africa deals. Some content is paywalled and the site blocks automated fetching, so fall back to search.
- **Ecofin Agency** — https://www.ecofinagency.com
  African business and economy wire. Fast on mining, energy and infrastructure deals; lighter on analysis.
- **Zambia Monitor** — https://zambiamonitor.com
  Zambian news site covering government, mining and Chinese investment. Pro-government tilt; cross-check contracts and figures.
- **News Diggers** — https://diggers.news
  Independent Zambian investigative outlet. Best local coverage of mining contracts, tax and corruption allegations.
- **Zitamar** — https://www.zitamar.com
  Mozambique-focused subscription service. Essential on Cabo Delgado, gas and Chinese involvement; paywalled.
- **Actualite.cd** — https://actualite.cd
  Leading independent DRC news site. Mining, provincial politics and security in the Copperbelt and Katanga.
- **NewsDay Zimbabwe** — https://www.newsday.co.zw
  Independent Zimbabwean daily. Lithium, Chinese investment and official statements.
- **Business Day** — https://www.businesslive.co.za
  South African business daily. Mining finance, corporate deals and Reserve Bank coverage.

## Think Tanks & Research Institutions (analytical depth)

These sources provide longer-form analysis, policy recommendations, and original research. Useful for context and deepening understanding, not for breaking news.

- **China-Global South Project** — https://www.chinaglobalsouth.com
  The specialist China-Africa newsroom: original reporting, data and interviews. Site blocks automated fetching; search for its latest pieces if extraction fails.
- **AidData** — https://www.aiddata.org
  Academic lab behind the TUFF dataset. Authoritative on Chinese financing amounts, lenders and project locations.
- **ECDPM** — https://ecdpm.org
  EU-Africa policy analysis. Critical of corridor economics and Western critical-minerals claims; useful for the European side.
- **SAIIA** — https://saiia.org.za
  South African Institute of International Affairs. China-Africa relations, minerals and South African foreign policy.
- **Institute for Security Studies** — https://issafrica.org
  Security research on organised crime, mining economies and insurgency links across the region.
- **CSIS Africa Program** — https://www.csis.org/regions/africa
  US policy framing on critical minerals, the Lobito Corridor and congressional debates.

## Official Sources (primary — biased, monitor for policy statements)

Direct statements from governments and state agencies. Essential for tracking policy positions, permits and financing. Treat as primary sources — the framing is always self-serving.

- **China Ministry of Commerce (MOFCOM)** — http://english.mofcom.gov.cn
  Chinese investment approvals, trade data and contract announcements.
- **Forum on China-Africa Cooperation (FOCAC)** — http://www.focac.org
  Summit commitments and follow-up. The source for pledged financing and policy promises.
- **African Union** — https://au.int
  Continental minerals and trade policy, including AfCFTA and the African Minerals Development Centre.
- **DRC Ministry of Mines** — https://mines.gouv.cd
  Copper and cobalt policy, export bans and strategic waivers.
- **Zimbabwe Investment and Development Agency (ZIDA)** — https://www.zidainvest.com
  Investment licences, special economic zones and Chinese project approvals.
- **Angola Ministry of Mineral Resources and Petroleum (MIREMPET)** — https://www.mirempet.gov.ao
  Oil, mining and Lobito corridor concessions.
- **US International Development Finance Corporation (DFC)** — https://www.dfc.gov
  US counter-finance: Lobito, critical minerals and project commitments.
- **US Treasury OFAC** — https://ofac.treasury.gov
  Sanctions designations affecting Chinese, Russian and African entities in the region.

## Advocacy & Civil Society (useful signal — biased framing)

These sources represent specific constituencies. Useful for tracking rights allegations, litigation and governance pressure. Always cross-reference with regional specialists.

- **Business & Human Rights Resource Centre** — https://www.business-humanrights.org
  Database of allegations against mining operations, including Chinese-owned sites. Strong on labour and community complaints.
- **Zimbabwe Environmental Law Association (ZELA)** — https://zela.org
  Zimbabwean legal and community advocacy. Litigation, environmental cases and benefit-sharing disputes.
- **Natural Resource Governance Institute (NRGI)** — https://resourcegovernance.org
  Contract transparency, revenue governance and comparative data on mineral-dependent states.

---

## Source Selection Guidance

1. **Check regional specialists first** — visit/extract each for new articles since the last run
2. **Then check official sources** — scan for new policy statements, permits, export rules or financing
3. **Then check think tanks** — for analytical context on recent developments
4. **Then check advocacy sources** — for rights allegations and litigation
5. **Finally, run Tavily searches** — to catch anything the curated sources missed
